## What was changed

Generated by [`pipeline/`](pipeline):

- **Male model:** about 2,200 BodyParts3D meshes classified into 128 named
  structures by FMA part-of membership and English name; cavities, hair and
  a few tiny ligaments dropped. Each structure's meshes were merged and
  decimated with meshoptimizer (about 6.4M → 1.0M triangles), converted from
  millimetres/Z-up to metres/Y-up, then quantized and compressed with
  `EXT_meshopt_compression`.
- **Joint hotspots** (shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle) are not
  meshes; their centres are computed from the adjoining bone ends and stored
  in the catalog.
- **Female model:** an average young adult MakeHuman female body, posed into
  the same relaxed anatomical pose as the male model and subdivided; the
  BodyParts3D anatomy fitted into it with a thin-plate-spline warp driven by
  about 600 matched landmarks; male-only structures dropped and the HRA female
  organs added (extracted, decimated and compressed).
